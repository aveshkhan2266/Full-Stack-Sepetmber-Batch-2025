Grandma's Classis Chocolate Chip Cookies

A simple, delicious, and timeless recipe for the perfect chocolate chip cookie

Recipe Details

Prep Time Cook Time Yield
15 mins 10 mins 24 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips

Instruction

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degreesF (175 degrees C).
  2. Cream together the butter, white sugar, and brown sugar until smooth.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
  4. Bake for about 10 minutes in the preheated oven, or until adges are nicely browned.
